# `zu` with Dative for Destinations and People

Imported draft; linguistic and CEFR claims have not been reverified during migration.

Use `zu` with the dative for going to a person or to a destination point without emphasizing entry into the interior.

## Pattern

`zu + dative`, often `zum` or `zur`

## Example

`Ich gehe zum Arzt.`
