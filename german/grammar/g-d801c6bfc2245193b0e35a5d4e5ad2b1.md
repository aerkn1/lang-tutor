# Contrast: `in` vs `zu` for Destination Meaning

Imported draft; linguistic and CEFR claims have not been reverified during migration.

`in` emphasizes entering and being inside a place, while `zu` emphasizes reaching a destination or meeting point.

## Pattern

`in + accusative` for entry, `zu + dative` for destination

## Example

`Ich gehe in den Supermarkt.` vs `Ich gehe zum Supermarkt.`
