# `sein` as the Perfekt Auxiliary for Movement and Change

Imported draft; linguistic and CEFR claims have not been reverified during migration.

In Perfekt, many movement and change-of-state verbs use `sein` as the helper verb instead of `haben`.

## Pattern

present-tense `sein` + past participle, for example `ich bin`, `du bist`, `er ist`, `wir sind`, `ihr seid`, `sie sind`

## Example

`Ich bin gefahren.`
