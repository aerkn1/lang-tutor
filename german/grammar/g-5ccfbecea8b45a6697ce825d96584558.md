# `in` with Accusative for Motion into a Bounded Space

Imported draft; linguistic and CEFR claims have not been reverified during migration.

Use `in` with the accusative when movement goes into an enclosed or bounded space.

## Pattern

`in + accusative` for direction into a place

## Example

`Ich gehe in den Park.`
