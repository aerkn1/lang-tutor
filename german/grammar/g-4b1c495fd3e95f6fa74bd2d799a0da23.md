# Definite Articles in the Dative

Imported draft; linguistic and CEFR claims have not been reverified during migration.

Definite articles change to mark the indirect object or a dative-governed phrase.

## Pattern

`der -> dem`, `das -> dem`, `die -> der`, plural `die -> den`

## Example

`Ich gehe zum Arzt.`
