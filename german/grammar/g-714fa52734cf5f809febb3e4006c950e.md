# Definite Articles in the Accusative

Imported draft; linguistic and CEFR claims have not been reverified during migration.

Definite articles change to mark the direct object, especially the masculine article.

## Pattern

`der -> den`, `die -> die`, `das -> das`

## Example

`Ich sehe den Mann.`
